# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / gate | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-001 | Kaggle identity verification is incomplete, potentially blocking participation or prize eligibility. | High | Critical | User completes Kaggle/Persona verification; verify status before submissions or compute spend. | Open |
| R-002 | No private GitHub source-of-truth or remote backup exists. | High | High | Private `Luvi-1` source-of-truth created and configured as `origin`; keep access restricted to recognized team members. | Resolved 2026-08-07 |
| R-003 | Fine-grained GitHub token currently has no private-repository visibility. | High | High | Grant only the approved repository after creation; verify `Luvi-1` before every write. | Open |
| R-004 | Only a small label subset is official; report-derived labels may be noisy, multilingual, and missing-not-at-random. | High | Critical | Unknown masks, manual validation, provenance, per-label confidence intervals, and versioned label card. | Open |
| R-005 | Patient/repeat/duplicate/report-template leakage could inflate validation. | High | Critical | Build identity and similarity groups before folds; audit every split. | Open |
| R-006 | Final notebook may exceed the nine-hour offline runtime. | Medium | Critical | Benchmark inference early and retain a substantial safety margin. | Open |
| R-007 | MIRA prohibits dataset redistribution, download-link sharing, re-identification, clinical use, and derivative works; required training and public winner artifacts may conflict. | Medium | Critical | Keep processing and artifacts private; seek written clarification; track private issues #5–#7. | Open |
| R-008 | Rules prohibit private code/data sharing outside the official Kaggle team. | Medium | Critical | Limit private repo access to recognized team members; do not invite collaborators before team/access review. | Open |
| R-009 | Winner-license text names CC-BY-NC 4.0 while also describing an OSI license that does not limit commercial use. | Medium | High | Treat the named license as noncommercial and not OSI-approved pending clarification; track private issue #3. | Open |
| R-010 | Public leaderboard overfitting could harm private rank. | Medium | High | Use locked OOF validation; treat leaderboard results as weak evidence only. | Open |
| R-011 | The local Python security policy ignores hidden editable-install path files. | Medium | Medium | Use locked non-editable installs via `UV_NO_EDITABLE=1` locally and in CI. | Mitigated |
| R-012 | Hidden scorer behavior is unspecified if a target has only one class. | Low | High | Require both classes per target in local evaluation; fail rather than skip, reweight, or impute. | Open |
| R-013 | The exact 86 retained DICOM tag names and scoring-time metadata availability are unpublished. | Medium | High | Do not make a metadata tag a model dependency until verified in the scoring-equivalent test interface. | Open |
| R-014 | Exact Kaggle GPU, RAM, TPU, and multi-accelerator limits are not stated on competition pages. | Medium | High | Assume one standard CPU/GPU only and revalidate live editor limits during notebook packaging. | Open |
| R-015 | Raw Kaggle file previews can expose identifiers or report text during metadata inspection. | Medium | Critical | Never open data previews for contract work; retain only aggregate counts, schemas, and hashes; stop and discard any accidental preview output. | Mitigated |
| R-016 | A stale competition contract could invalidate submissions or experiments. | Medium | Critical | Refresh with official sources and run the material drift checker before modeling or packaging. | Mitigated |
| R-017 | The public competition start and API activation timestamp disagree. | Low | Medium | Track private issue #9; derive all operational gates from the independently verified deadlines. | Open |
| R-018 | Published code licenses do not necessarily cover checkpoints, pretrained weights, external datasets, or derived artifacts. | High | Critical | Keep every discovered asset blocked until exact version, URL, license, checksum, equal-access evidence, offline packaging, MIRA compatibility, and approval are recorded. | Open |
| R-019 | Hospital, scanner, field strength, protocol, prevalence, report language, and template are entangled and may reverse model rankings. | High | Critical | Use them first as protected groups and stress-test strata; require every mandatory domain to meet support and use the canonical protected-group Bayesian-bootstrap interval. | Open |
| R-020 | Report-derived weak labels may have target-, language-, template-, and severity-dependent omissions rather than class-conditional noise. | High | Critical | Preserve positive/negative/uncertain/unmentioned, perform paired report-versus-image audit, stratify error, and use only fold-contained aggregation. | Open |
| R-021 | Decode, AMP, compile, export, caching, or slice-budget optimizations may change geometry, pixels, ordering, or predictions while appearing faster. | Medium | Critical | Establish eager FP32 full-wall baseline; require syntax/pixel/preprocessing/prediction parity and all-label non-inferiority; retain deterministic fallback. | Open |
| R-022 | Public notebooks and participant claims can be stale, copied, unlicensed, poorly validated, or overfit to the public 30%. | High | High | Timestamp landscape evidence; separate official facts from participant claims; never copy or select from public scores alone. | Open |
