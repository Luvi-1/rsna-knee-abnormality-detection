## Chunk 5 gates

- Do not depend on any specific DICOM metadata tag being present in the scoring interface merely
  because it appears in training data; the exact allowlist remains unpublished.
- Treat `ImageOrientationPatient`, `ImagePositionPatient`, spacing, photometric interpretation,
  rescale values, transfer syntax, and laterality defensively. Missing or contradictory geometry
  must use a documented flagged fallback or block the series; it may not be silently imputed.
- The official transfer-syntax inventory includes uncompressed little-endian, JPEG Lossless, and
  JPEG 2000 data. Every decoder path requires explicit compatibility and failure evidence.
- Do not resample thick-slice data as if it contained isotropic detail or introduce horizontal
  flips without a separately verified anatomical label transform.
- Full-data and case-level QA stay on Kaggle. Local execution is restricted to code, synthetic
  fixtures, public metadata, and disclosure-controlled aggregates.
- Actual Kaggle CPU allocation, RAM, and accelerator details remain unresolved. Benchmark the
  allocated private runtime rather than assuming a hardware profile.
- Any unexplained decode failure, ambiguous ordering, inconsistent orientation, lost laterality,
  checksum mismatch, nondeterminism, or systematic visual-QA error blocks preprocessing and all
  downstream work.
